# Contributing to the Open Education Suite  
*A Charter of the Guild of Learners and Makers*

Welcome, traveler.  
By opening this scroll, you stand at the threshold of the Guild — a fellowship devoted to **craftsmanship**, **clarity**, **discipline**, and **stewardship**.  
Your contributions strengthen not only the code, but the community that inherits it.

This charter outlines the expectations, virtues, and rituals of contribution.

---

## **The Four Virtues of Contribution**

### **1. Discipline — The Forge of Consistent Craft**
- **Submit work that is complete, tested, and intentional**  
- **Follow existing patterns unless improvement is clear and documented**  
- **Keep commits focused and meaningful**  

### **2. Clarity — The Lantern of Understanding**
- **Write code others can read without deciphering**  
- **Document decisions, not just functions**  
- **Name things with precision and purpose**  

### **3. Creativity — The Quill of Innovation**
- **Propose new modules, tools, or study paths**  
- **Experiment boldly in branches, refine humbly in pull requests**  
- **Share ideas that expand the Guild’s horizons**  

### **4. Stewardship — The Oath of the Guild**
- **Leave the repository better than you found it**  
- **Review others’ work with generosity and rigor**  
- **Honor the open‑source spirit: transparency, respect, and service**  

---

## **How to Contribute**

### **1. Choose Your Path**
You may contribute:
- **Tools** (PDF → Anki pipelines, scrapers, summarizers)  
- **Study plans** (templates, domain‑specific paths)  
- **Curated resources** (textbooks, courses, practice sites)  
- **Documentation** (guides, workflows, architecture notes)  
- **Guild lore** (virtue frameworks, onboarding rituals)  

### **2. Prepare Your Work**
- Ensure your code runs cleanly  
- Add or update documentation  
- Follow the repository’s directory structure  
- Include examples when relevant  

### **3. Submit a Pull Request**
A pull request is a **petition to the Guild Council**.  
It should include:
- Purpose of the change  
- The virtue(s) it strengthens  
- Any tradeoffs or design decisions  

### **4. Participate in Review**
Reviews are conversations, not judgments.  
Offer insight. Receive correction. Strengthen the craft.

---

## **Guild Etiquette**
- **Honor the time of others** — keep discussions focused  
- **Assume good intent** — we are all apprentices of mastery  
- **Preserve thematic consistency** — the Guild aesthetic matters  
- **Uphold the virtues** — they are the pillars of this suite  

---

## **Final Oath**
By contributing, you affirm:

> *“I build not for myself alone,  
> but for those who will follow the path I leave behind.”*

Welcome to the Guild, apprentice or master.  
Your work becomes part of our shared legacy.
